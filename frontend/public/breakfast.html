Bias (9:20)

  • NIFTY50 first 5m close vs open.
  • Flat (0%) → Long branch.
  • Negative → Short branch.

Sectors

  • Rank 16 Nifty sector indices on first 5m %.
  • Discard sectors with no F&O stocks in arbitrage_master.
  • Top 1 sector (long) or bottom 1 (short).

Stocks

  • Rank by % vs prev close at 9:20.
  • Long: 0% < move < 4%.
  • Short: −4% < move < 0%.
  • Top 2 in sector; nearest FUT (e.g. SEP26) or SPOT.

Exit

  • Anchor = 9:15 bar close (TP/SL ±1%); entry ±0.03% slippage.
  • Stock rank uses 9:20 close vs prev day.
  • Monitor from 9:20 (9:15 entry) or 9:25; optional ₹5K cap.
  • VWAP breach on close; 10:15 time exit if still open.
